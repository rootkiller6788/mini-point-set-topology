MiniTopologicalSpaces Changelog
==================================

v0.1.0 (2026-05-21)
  - Initial standalone mini-package with full skeleton
  - Core: TopologicalSpace via open sets, OpenSet, ClosedSet, Neighborhood,
          Interior, Closure, Boundary, LimitPoint, IsolatedPoint, Dense,
          Separable, discrete/indiscrete topologies, Object instances
  - Laws: Open/closed duality, Kuratowski closure axioms (partial), interior/closure
  - Morphisms: ContinuousMap, OpenMap, ClosedMap, QuotientMap, Embedding,
               Homeomorphism, HomotopyEquivalence, WeakHomotopyEquivalence
  - Constructions: Product topology, quotient topology, subspace topology,
                   Subbasis/Basis, universal properties
  - Properties: FirstCountable, SecondCountable, Lindelof, Compact, Connected,
                preservation theorems, finite topology classification
  - Theorems: Tychonoff (binary + general), unique limits in Hausdorff,
              classification of 1-manifolds, Alexander subbase, 14-set theorem
              (most proofs marked with sorry)
  - Examples: Euclidean, discrete, indiscrete, cofinite, cocountable,
              Sierpinski space, Sorgenfrey line, counterexamples
  - Bridges: Topological groups/rings/vector spaces, metric spaces,
             metrizability, topological manifolds, charts/atlases,
             finite topological spaces, digital topology
  - Tests: Smoke, Examples, Regression
  - Benchmarks: CoreCoverage, Princeton, CambridgePartIII, Harvard, MIT, OxfordPartC
  - Computation: notebooks, python, sage stubs, run_compute, topology_data
